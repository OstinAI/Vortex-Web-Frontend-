📘 KAK USTANOVIT INDIKATOR "KOLICHESTVO SOTRUDNIKOV"

1. Skopiruyte papku /crm/indicator/number of employees/ v vash proekt.
2. Podklyuchite employees-count.css v <head> stranicy:
<link rel="stylesheet" href="/crm/indicator/number of employees/employees-count.css">

3. Podklyuchite employees-count.js pered zakrytiem <body>:
<script src="/crm/indicator/number of employees/employees-count.js"></script>

4. Vstavte HTML-kod indikatora v nuzhnoe mesto:
<div class="crm-indicator employees-count-button animated vortex-style"
     data-action="employees-count" data-href="/employees">
     <div class="indicator-content">
         <span class="employees-icon">
             <img src="/images/population.gif"
                 alt="Сотрудники" data-gif="true">
         </span>
         <span class="count-number">42</span>
         <span class="count-label">сотр.</span>
     </div>
</div>

5. 🎬 РАБОТА С ГИФКОЙ:
  • По умолчанию гифка СТАТИЧНА (показывает первый кадр)
  • При НАВЕДЕНИИ мыши гифка проигрывается непрерывно
  • При уходе мыши гифка снова становится статичной

6. Dlya obnovleniya kolichestva cherez JS:
window.EmployeesCountIndicator.refresh(123);
// ili bez parametra — podtyanet iz localStorage

7. Klyuchi dlya polucheniya dannyh iz localStorage:
employees_count, users_count, staff_count

8. Gifka dolzhna lezhat po puti:
/images/population.gif

✅ Indikator gotov k ispolzovaniyu na lyuboi stranice!
